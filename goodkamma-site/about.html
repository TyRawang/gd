About us

Good Kamma Inc. is an independent Canadian company. We build and operate businesses in commerce and technology, and we back new ideas we believe in.

What we do

We start, run, and grow small businesses. Our work spans online retail, software, and services, and we take each one from first idea through day-to-day operations.

We keep our teams lean and our tools practical, so each business can stand on its own and serve its customers well.

How we work

We take a long-term view. We would rather build something steady that lasts than chase quick wins, and we measure success by the trust we earn from customers, partners, and the people we work with.

  • We say what we will do, then do it.
  • We treat customers and partners fairly.
  • We reinvest in what works and stop what does not.

Our name

Kamma is the Pali word for karma: the idea that what we do comes back to us. It is a simple reminder to act with care, because good work and good conduct tend to return in kind.

Where we are

Good Kamma Inc. is based in Alberta, Canada, and works with customers and partners across the country.
